About
Vendela engages into autoethnographic exploration, weaving personal themes of trauma into her artistic practice to communicate the unspoken. She seeks to evoke emotional response beyond mere comprehension or conversation. Transforming vulnerabilities into sources of empowerment, she uses art as a means of reclaiming agency over her experiences. Focusing on sound-based installations, Vendela crafts immersive auditory sculptures. Her sonic creations invite the audience to not only listen but also to feel, recognizing the profound impact sound can have.
